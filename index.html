Research and implementation in partnership with Fundația Conservation Carpathia in Romania's Făgăraș Mountains

Preprint · September 2026 · under review

Geospatial embeddings detect old-growth forests but buffered spatial validation narrows their advantage over Sentinel features

Foundation-model embeddings and ordinary Sentinel features can detect old-growth forest. However, once training and test sites are kept 10 km apart, the embeddings' advantage is no longer distinguishable from zero. This paper shows why accounting for spatial autocorrelation matters for old-growth forest mapping.

Using AI to help rangers protect wildlife and rural communities

A two-stage classifier identifies wildlife, such as bears, wolves and wild boar, in photos received from internet-enabled camera traps, then sends real-time alerts to Fundația Conservation Carpathia's rapid intervention teams. Deployed without interruption since May 2024.

Experience

My work sits between research, engineering and policy. At Cambridge, I apply machine learning and remote sensing to environmental problems, from detecting old-growth forests to automating wildlife monitoring. Previously, I led teams of technical specialists and programme managers in DR Congo, Sierra Leone and Tanzania and secured over £85 million for infrastructure, investment and job creation programmes.

  1. 2023–

    PhD + MRes in AI for Environmental Risk, University of Cambridge

    Old-growth forest detection from satellite data and geospatial foundation models, and real-time wildlife alerts from camera traps, in partnership with Fundația Conservation Carpathia.

  2. 2020–2023

    Head of the Private Sector Development Hub, British High Commission, Tanzania

    Led the UK's partnership with Tanzania on climate finance and private sector development. Diplomatic engagement ahead of COP26, after which Tanzania doubled its mitigation target and removed coal from its energy plan. Secured a £35m green growth programme. Ran a pilot using AI on satellite imagery to detect mangrove loss in the Rufiji Delta.

  3. 2017–2020

    Deputy Team Leader, Economic Growth, British High Commission, Sierra Leone

    Pivoted the UK's post-Ebola economic partnership towards long-term growth and jobs. Designed and mobilised Invest Salone (£30m) for agribusiness, tourism and fisheries. Oversaw a $50m public-private partnership to build and run 100 solar mini-grids.

    Managed a £70m portfolio of agriculture, renewable energy and public works programmes, including one of the UK's largest market systems programmes, which has reached an estimated 979,000 people. Redesigned a £20m road programme in the conflict-affected east; construction employed 2,000 people.
