Selected work

Research and implementation in partnership with Fundația Conservation Carpathia in Romania's Făgăraș Mountains

Preprint · September 2026 · under review

Geospatial embeddings detect old-growth forests but buffered spatial validation narrows their advantage over Sentinel features

Foundation-model embeddings and ordinary Sentinel features can detect old-growth forests. However, once training and test sites are kept 10 km apart, the embeddings' initial advantage is less clear. This paper shows why spatial relationships matter when mapping old-growth forests.

Deployed · 2024 · real-time wildlife alerts

Using AI to help rangers protect wildlife and rural communities

A two-stage AI classifier identifies wildlife, such as bears, wolves and wild boar, in photos received from internet-enabled camera traps. The system then sends real-time alerts to Fundația Conservation Carpathia's rapid intervention teams. In operational use since May 2024.

Experience

My work sits between research, engineering and policy. I spent ten years with the UK Government, including postings to British embassies and high commissions in DR Congo, Sierra Leone and Tanzania. I led teams of technical specialists and programme managers and secured over £85 million for infrastructure, climate and job-creation programmes. I returned to Cambridge in 2023 to specialise in machine learning and remote sensing, leading research on old-growth forest detection and deploying an AI-based wildlife monitoring system that has been in operational use since 2024.

  1. 2023–

    PhD and MRes, Centre for Doctoral Training in AI for Environmental Risks, University of Cambridge

    Old-growth forest detection from satellite data and geospatial foundation models, and real-time wildlife alerts from camera traps, in partnership with Fundația Conservation Carpathia. Lead author of a study testing whether geospatial foundation models (AlphaEarth, TESSERA) beat conventional Sentinel-1/2 features for finding old-growth forests. Talks at ML4EO 2026 (Exeter, UK) and the Joint NSO–GfÖ Conference 2026 (Odense, Denmark). Undergraduate supervision in remote sensing and quantitative methods.

  2. 2024–

    Expert Committee Member, Biodiversity Challenge Funds

    Strategic and funding advice for the Darwin Initiative, the UK's grant scheme for biodiversity and local communities.

  3. 2020–2023

    Head of the Private Sector Development Hub, British High Commission, Tanzania

    Led the UK's partnership with Tanzania on climate finance and private sector development. Diplomatic engagement ahead of COP26, after which Tanzania doubled its mitigation target and removed coal from its energy plan. Secured a £35m green growth programme. Ran a pilot using AI on satellite imagery to detect mangrove loss in the Rufiji Delta.

  4. 2017–2020

    Deputy Team Leader, Economic Growth, British High Commission, Sierra Leone

    Pivoted the UK's post-Ebola economic partnership towards long-term growth and jobs. Designed and mobilised Invest Salone (£30m) for agribusiness, tourism and fisheries. Oversaw a US$50m public-private partnership to build and run 100 solar mini-grids.

  5. 2015–2017

    Private Sector Development Adviser, British Embassy, Democratic Republic of Congo

    Managed a £70m portfolio of agriculture, renewable energy and public works programmes, including one of the UK's largest market systems programmes, which has reached an estimated 979,000 people. Redesigned a £20m road programme in the conflict-affected east; construction employed 2,000 people.

  6. 2013–2015

  8. 2010–2012

    Financial models across the major asset classes; administered a £240m derivative fund. Completed the CFA Program (2012); awarded the CFA charter (2021).
